# BYJU'S Spend Review Chatbot

A source-cited assistant for reviewing marketing, sponsorship, and expansion proposals. It flags risks and missing information; human reviewers make the final decision.

## Run

Open index.html in a browser. No installation is needed. For the proposal form and 12 stress tests, open proposal-review.html.

## Evidence and privacy

See web-evidence-pack.md for sources and limitations. Public evidence cannot establish a proposal's ROI or replace approved internal data. Do not enter personal or confidential information. Reviews stay in the browser and are not saved or sent to a server.
