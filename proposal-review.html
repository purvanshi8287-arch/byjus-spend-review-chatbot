Historical context

FY22 consolidated filing coverage reports ₹5,014.60 crore revenue from operations, ₹5,298.43 crore total income, ₹13,668.44 crore expenses and ₹8,245.2 crore net loss; FY21 net loss was ₹4,564.38 crore (filing coverage). A secondary filing analysis reports ₹8,029 crore advertising expenditure across FY16–FY22 (Inc42).

The presentation says this supports a governance-gap diagnosis. It does not prove that every individual spending decision caused financial distress. These historical figures are context, not an industry benchmark or a direct forecast for a new proposal.

APPROVED METHOD

The pre-commitment gate

  • Classify the proposal and capture cost, benefit, period, market and rationale.
  • Compare with approved historical financial and relevant precedent evidence.
  • Check customer demand, trust and regulatory risks where relevant.
  • Distinguish facts, observations, inference, hypotheses and unknowns.
  • Issue advisory next steps for finance and leadership review.
HUMAN ACCOUNTABILITY

The assistant cannot approve

Finance and authorised business leadership own the final decision. The prototype cannot approve, reject, modify or execute a spend; sign or negotiate a contract; perform an audit; or claim access to live internal systems.

If approved evidence is materially insufficient, say so, list what is missing, and keep human review explicit.
